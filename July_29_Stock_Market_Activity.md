# Stock Market Activity Summary - July 29, 2025

## Overview

The stock market closed mixed on Tuesday, July 29, 2025, as investors digested a flurry of earnings reports and awaited the Federal Reserve's interest rate decision. The day was characterized by a significant selloff in chip/semiconductor stocks amid concerns over slowing AI infrastructure spending.

## Major Index Performance

- **S&P 500**: Fell approximately 1.5%, closing at 6,370.86 (down 0.30%)
- **Nasdaq Composite**: Declined 0.4% to 21,098.29, entering a technical correction after extending its slide from record highs by 11%
- **Dow Jones Industrial Average**: Slipped 0.5%, closing at 44,632.99 (down 204.57 points)

## Key Drivers

### 1. Semiconductor Selloff

The primary driver of the day's volatility was a continued rout in chipmakers and semiconductor stocks. Investors expressed concerns over:

- Slowing AI infrastructure spending
- Weak earnings from major tech companies
- Rising geopolitical tensions affecting supply chains

Notable semiconductor companies included Micron Technology (MU), AMD, Marvell Technology (MRVL), Lam Research (LRCX), Qualcomm (QCOM), and Nvidia. The iShares Semiconductor ETF saw a 3.8% decline that day, bringing its week-to-date losses to over 10%.

### 2. Federal Reserve Decision

The market was positioned ahead of the Fed's 2:00 p.m. ET policy decision on July 29, where rates were expected to remain steady at 3.5%-3.75%. Key concerns included:

- Financial conditions continuing to tighten
- Rising long-dated Treasury yields (the 30-year yield hit the highest level since 2007)
- Fed rate hike expectations moving higher

### 3. Earnings Reports

A busy week of earnings reports weighed on investor sentiment, with major companies including:

- Microsoft (MSFT)
- Meta Platforms (META)
- Procter & Gamble (PG)
- Vertiv (VRT)
- General Dynamics (GD)
- Aon (AON)

### 4. Geopolitical Tensions

Renewed tensions in the Middle East contributed to market volatility, with oil prices rising nearly 7% before the Fed decision. This added pressure on equities and contributed to the broader market weakness.

## Market Context

The S&P 500 snapped a six-day streak of record closing highs, while the Nasdaq's four-session winning run ended. The Dow Jones Industrial Average had previously added 1% on July 28th before the selloff intensified on Wednesday.

## Aftermarket Impact

Equity-index futures for Australia, Japan, and South Korea all pointed lower following the S&P 500's decline, indicating that the chipmaker rout was expected to continue into international markets.
